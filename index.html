Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They are known for their large ears, long trunks, tusks, and strong social bonds. Elephants are highly intelligent animals and often live together in family groups.

Elephants are herbivores, meaning they mainly eat plants, grass, fruits, and tree bark. They use their trunks to pick up food, drink water, and communicate with other elephants.
